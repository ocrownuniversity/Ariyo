The story behind Fluent Futures Jax.

Our founder

[Founder name placeholder] is an Atlanta native who relocated to Jacksonville in 2022. Their upbringing was deeply shaped by the Civil Rights Movement, historic Black communities, competitive step, marching band, HBCU experiences, and mentorship.

The historical connections between Atlanta and Jacksonville, particularly through James Weldon Johnson, have also influenced the educational philosophy behind Fluent Futures Jax.

While these experiences shaped the vision, the organization remains centered on Jacksonville, its young people, and the communities it hopes to serve.
